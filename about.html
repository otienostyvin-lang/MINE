My Approach To Every Project

When working on a project, i follow a simple process:

1.Understanding the Goal

I first take time to understand the purpose of the website,the target audience ,and the goals of the client.

2.Planning the Structure

I organize the pages,contents,sections, and user journey before development begins.

3.Designing the Experience

I focus on creating a clean,attractive and easy-to-use interface.

4.Developing the Website

I use modern frontend technologies to transform the design into a functional and responsive website.

5.Testing and Improving

Before launching a project , i test the website accross different screen sizes and make improvements where necessary.
